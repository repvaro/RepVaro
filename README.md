# RepVaro

**Gym management software built for small and mid-sized gyms in India.**

RepVaro replaces paper registers, spreadsheets, and scattered WhatsApp
messages with a single dashboard for member management, attendance,
payments, and automated renewal reminders.

🔗 [repvaro.com](https://repvaro.com) · 🆓 [15-day free trial](https://repvaro.com/trial)

## Why RepVaro

Most gym owners in India are still running their business out of a
notebook and a phone full of manually-typed reminder texts. Big
international gym software is overpriced and built for chains with
back-office teams. RepVaro is built specifically for the owner who's
on the gym floor all day, not behind a desk — with zero training
required to use it.

## What it does

- **👥 Member Management** — searchable profiles, membership history,
  automatic inactive-member cleanup.
- **✅ Attendance Tracking** — one-tap check-in, full history, early
  churn detection.
- **💳 Memberships & Payments** — unlimited custom plans, renewal
  management, pending-dues tracking, daily/monthly/yearly income.
- **🔔 Automatic WhatsApp Reminders** — expiry reminders, payment
  confirmations, trial welcomes, and owner subscription alerts, all
  via approved WhatsApp Business templates.
- **📊 Dashboard & Reports** — live view of active members, expiring
  memberships, attendance, and collections.
- **☁️ Cloud Backups & Security** — nightly encrypted backups with
  checksums, Google Drive export, point-in-time recovery, and weekly
  restore drills.
- **⚙️ Built for Real Operations** — multi-tenant architecture,
  scheduled background jobs (IST), CSRF protection, and internal
  health checks — not just a demo.
- **📱 Works Anywhere** — mobile-first, installable like an app, no
  app store needed.

## Who it's for

- Neighborhood gyms that need a clear view of members, payments, and
  renewals without enterprise complexity.
- Growing gyms that need automation and reminders without hiring
  extra admin staff.
- Any owner who's ever lost track of who's paid, who's expiring, or
  who stopped showing up.
